Methodology

Rightmove searched live on 25 Sep 2026 via locationIdentifier=REGION^<id> with radius=3.0, propertyTypes=detached, minBedrooms=3, maxBedrooms=4, maxPrice=500000, paged to exhaustion. 225 results → 146 unique → 139 after removing Ambrosden and anything over the cap. 41 full detail pages were decoded and re-scored on complete key-features and description text, then 17 shortlisted and hand-reviewed line by line.

Scoring. The n/6 badge counts only criteria confirmed in writing in the listing. Hedged language — "potential to create", "could be converted", "scope for", or a utility cupboard/area rather than a room — is recorded as an amber ~ chip with a hover tooltip and reported as a +N~ suffix. Partials never count toward the score.
