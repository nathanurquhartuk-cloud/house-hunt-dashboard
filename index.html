Method & sources

Searches run live on Rightmove on 4 September 2026 for each of the seven areas (1.0-mile radius, detached only, 3–4 beds, max £700,000), de-duplicated by listing ID, with every Ambrosden address removed. Each of the 222 unique homes was scored out of 6 against the must-have list using its full listing text and key features: ✓ confirmed · ~ partial or convertible · ✗ not evidenced. The Chesterton search was pinned to Chesterton, Bicester, Oxfordshire after the plain "Chesterton" slug was found to resolve to Chesterton, Newcastle-under-Lyme, Staffordshire.

Recommended offers are derived from Rightmove sold-price records for each outcode (detached, last 2 years): OX26 median £425k (IQR £330k–£510k, n=147) · NN13 median £400k (£350k–£550k, n=147) · OX7 median £555k (£400k–£680k, n=138) · OX2 median £633k (£423k–£929k, n=120) · OX1 median £521k (£395k–£735k, n=114) · OX25 median £530k (£440k–£673k, n=135) — then adjusted for floor area, specification, weeks on market, prior reductions, chain-free status and new-build incentives.

Cross-portal links found by street-name match against OnTheMarket and Zoopla. All 14 homes on last week's shortlist were re-checked against a Sold-STC-inclusive search: every one is still available and none has changed price.

⚠️ This is research, not valuation advice. Prices were read live on 4 September 2026 and may have changed since. Always verify with viewings, a surveyor and your own solicitor before making an offer.
