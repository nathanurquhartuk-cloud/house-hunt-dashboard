Methodology

Rightmove was searched live on 2 Oct 2026 using locationIdentifier=REGION^<id> with radius=3.0, propertyTypes=detached, 3–4 beds, maxPrice=500000 and includeSSTC=true, paged to exhaustion. That gave 399 rows, then 262 unique listings after removing duplicates, Ambrosden and anything over the cap. Of those, 142 were Available, not plots. All 142 detail pages were decoded and auto-scored on the full key features and description, then the top candidates were re-read by hand and 16 shortlisted.

Scoring. The n/6 badge counts only criteria confirmed in writing in the listing. Hedged language ("potential to create", "could be used as", "scope for"), a combined cloakroom/utility, or a converted garage is recorded as an amber ~ chip with a hover tooltip and shown as a +N~ suffix. Partials never count toward the score.
