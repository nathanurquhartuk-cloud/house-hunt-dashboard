Methodology

Listings read live from Rightmove on 16 October 2026 (3.0 mile radius per area, detached, 3–4 beds, £500,000 maximum; Ambrosden excluded). Scores count only criteria confirmed in the listing text; wording such as "potential", "could be" or a utility area is shown as an amber ~ and is not counted. Availability comes from Rightmove display status (two properties from last week, Orion Drive Brackley and one other, are now Sold STC and have been removed). Opening offers and ceilings are judgement calls from outcode sold prices (last 2 years, all detached sizes: OX26 £425k · OX25 £530k · OX15 £472k · OX14 £453k · NN13 £455k · OX7 £525k · OX27 £460k · OX5 £460k · OX1 £500k · OX2 £525k medians), adjusted for size, spec, time on market, reductions and fixed-price terms; ceilings never exceed the advertised price or £500,000.
